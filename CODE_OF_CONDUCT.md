# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (short version)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in verset-finder a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- **Do:** use welcoming language, respect viewpoints, accept constructive criticism, show empathy.
- **Don't:** use sexualized language/imagery, troll, insult, harass publicly or privately, or publish others' private information.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers (open a private security advisory or
contact via the emails on the GitHub profile). Maintainers will review, may remove
offending content, and may ban repeat offenders. All complaints stay confidential.
